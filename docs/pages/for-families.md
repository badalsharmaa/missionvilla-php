## Section 02: Family-Decision Guide (`for-families.decision-guide`)

* **Section Key:** `ff-decision-guide`
* **Status:** Enabled
* **Order:** 20
* **Eyebrow:** Family-Decision Guide
* **Main Heading:** How to Consider Full-Time Memory Care vs. *Temporary Respite Care*
* **Description:** Mission Villa focuses exclusively on two interconnected offerings in Daly City: ongoing Legacies™ Memory Care and short-term Respite Care. Select the reflection below that sounds closest to your family’s current conversation.

### 4 Family Reflection Pathways

1. **Pathway 01 · Ongoing Daily Living & Memory Support — When Daily Routines & Memory Changes Call for Steady Community Support**
   - *Family Situation:* “My parent or spouse is living with Alzheimer’s disease or dementia, and our family wants a calm, nurturing community where they are known personally and supported around the clock.”
   - *Primary Option:* Memory Care (Legacies™ Program) → Explore Legacies™ Memory Care (`/memory-care`)
   - *How to Think About It:* Full-time Memory Care at Mission Villa is designed for older adults who benefit from consistent daily rhythms, 24-hour on-site staff presence, and person-centered support rooted in their personal life story.
   - *Key Signs of Fit:*
     - Your loved one benefits from predictable morning, mealtime, and evening routines in a homelike environment
     - Hands-on daily assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, or escorting would improve everyday comfort and dignity
     - Social connection, chef-prepared dining, and tailored Legacies™ activities would reduce isolation at home
     - Family caregivers want to shift from round-the-clock care coordination back to meaningful visits as spouse, son, or daughter
   - *How Both Programs Connect:* Some families begin with a personal walkthrough of Memory Care suites right away, while others arrange a short-term Respite Care stay first so their loved one can experience our dining room and daily rhythm firsthand.
   - *Suggested First Question:* How does the Legacies™ Memory Care team learn my loved one’s personal history and help them settle into the first few weeks?

2. **Pathway 02 · Caregiver Rest, Travel or Family Obligations — When a Primary Family Caregiver Needs Dependable Time to Rest or Travel**
   - *Family Situation:* “I care for my loved one at home and want them welcomed into a safe, compassionate community for a few days or several weeks while I recharge, travel, or handle family commitments.”
   - *Primary Option:* Short-Term Respite Care → Explore Short-Term Respite Care (`/respite-care`)
   - *How to Think About It:* Respite Care offers a temporary, furnished stay (subject to current availability) with the same attentive staff support, chef-prepared meals, and engaging daily activities enjoyed by full-time residents.
   - *Key Signs of Fit:*
     - You have upcoming travel, work obligations, or family events and want dependable 24-hour support for your loved one
     - As a dedicated family caregiver, you need intentional time to rest and protect your own wellbeing
     - Your loved one would enjoy sociable meals, activities, and a comfortable suite with a private bathroom during your time away
     - You want direct communication with our Daly City team while you are away
   - *How Both Programs Connect:* Many families use Respite Care periodically throughout the year for caregiver rest, and find it provides natural familiarity if full-time Memory Care is ever needed later.
   - *Suggested First Question:* What short-term stay lengths and furnished suite accommodations are currently available for Respite Care?

3. **Pathway 03 · Bridge Support After Illness or Rehabilitation — When Returning Straight Home Feels Too Soon After a Health Change**
   - *Family Situation:* “After an illness, hospital stay, or rehabilitation period, our family wants a supportive environment where daily meals, personal care, and routines are looked after before deciding next steps.”
   - *Primary Option:* Short-Term Respite Care → Review Respite Care Transitions (`/respite-care`)
   - *How to Think About It:* As noted on Mission Villa’s Respite Care page, a short-term stay can offer extra daily support after illness or rehabilitation—giving your loved one attentive daily assistance, regular meals, and unhurried recovery time.
   - *Key Signs of Fit:*
     - Your family needs time to prepare the home or evaluate whether returning home is practical
     - Consistent daily meals, housekeeping, laundry, and personal care assistance would ease the transition
     - Routine assessments help clarify what level of ongoing daily support feels most comfortable
     - You want an unhurried window to observe how your loved one responds to community life
   - *How Both Programs Connect:* If a guest thrives in the community rhythm during a transitional Respite Care stay, our team can discuss long-term Memory Care suite options without any high-pressure obligation.
   - *Suggested First Question:* How does your pre-stay assessment coordinate with our family when planning a transitional Respite Care stay?

4. **Pathway 04 · Early Exploration & Suite Comparison — When Your Family Is Gathering Information Before an Urgent Crisis**
   - *Family Situation:* “We are beginning first conversations as a family and want to understand Memory Care vs. Respite Care, compare Private and Companion suites, and learn how costs work without sales pressure.”
   - *Primary Option:* Comparing Both Programs → Compare Suites & Floor Plans (`/suites-and-floor-plans`)
   - *How to Think About It:* Visiting before an urgent need allows siblings, spouses, and relatives to walk through our Daly City community calmly, meet staff, see suites in person, and align on financial planning at a comfortable pace.
   - *Key Signs of Fit:*
     - Multiple family members are sharing the decision and want clear, consistent facts to discuss together
     - You want to see the difference between a Private Suite, a Companion Unit, and a Jack & Jill layout in person
     - You want to understand how assessment-informed pricing and home-expense comparisons work early on
     - You value a pressure-free conversation where questions are welcomed
   - *How Both Programs Connect:* An early visit lets your family learn about both ongoing Legacies™ Memory Care and short-term Respite Care so you know every option available at 995 East Market Street.
   - *Suggested First Question:* May our family schedule an informational walkthrough to see suites, dining spaces, and review how pricing is structured?

### Side-by-Side Comparison Dimensions
1. **Primary Purpose & Focus:** *Memory Care:* Ongoing, residential support for seniors living with Alzheimer’s disease or other forms of dementia through our signature Legacies™ Memory Care program. | *Respite Care:* Temporary, short-term stays that provide attentive care for a senior guest while giving primary family caregivers time to rest, travel, or manage obligations. | *Takeaway:* Both programs share the same compassionate staff, homelike Daly City setting, and respect for personal dignity.
2. **Typical Time Horizon:** *Memory Care:* Long-term community residency shaped around familiar, unhurried daily rhythms and evolving personal care plans. | *Respite Care:* Short-term stays ranging from a few days to several weeks (confirm current stay duration terms and availability with our team). | *Takeaway:* A short-term Respite stay can also serve as a gentle introduction before deciding on longer-term residency.
3. **Suites & Living Spaces:** *Memory Care:* Private Suites, Companion Units, and Jack & Jill floor-plan formats—each featuring a private bathroom and space for personal furniture and keepsakes. | *Respite Care:* Comfortable, furnished short-term accommodations with private bathroom access and full enjoyment of communal lounges, dining room, and courtyard. | *Takeaway:* Even for a short stay, bringing a few familiar photos or a favorite blanket helps a suite feel immediately welcoming.
4. **Dining, Activities & Support:** *Memory Care:* 24-hour staff availability, personalized help with daily tasks, three chef-prepared meals daily, housekeeping, laundry, and Legacies™ life-enrichment programming. | *Respite Care:* Tailored daily assistance, three chef-prepared meals and snacks daily, housekeeping, and warm inclusion in daily social, creative, and recreational activities. | *Takeaway:* Respite Care guests are welcomed into the heart of daily community life—never set apart.
5. **How Pricing Is Discussed:** *Memory Care:* Assessment-informed monthly pricing based on your loved one’s personal care needs and chosen suite format (Private, Companion, or Jack & Jill). | *Respite Care:* Arranged based on desired stay dates, furnished suite availability, and daily support needs confirmed during a pre-stay conversation. | *Takeaway:* Our team shares clear, individualized cost information directly with your family—without generic online calculators.

## Section 03: Questions to Bring to a Visit (`for-families.checklist`)

* **Section Key:** `ff-checklist`
* **Status:** Enabled
* **Order:** 30
* **Eyebrow:** Preparing for Your Walkthrough
* **Main Heading:** Questions to Bring to a Visit in Daly City
* **Description:** Walking into a senior living community for the first time is much easier when you have a clear set of questions in hand. Check the questions below that matter most to your family—then click **Bring Selected Questions to Visit Form** so our team can prepare thoughtful answers for your visit.

### 8 Tour Checklist Questions
1. **Q01 · Daily Care & Legacies™:** How does the Legacies™ Memory Care team learn about my loved one’s life story, habits, and personal preferences before move-in?
   - *Why it matters:* Person-centered care depends on knowing who your loved one has always been—their family bonds, career, favorite music, and comforting routines.
   - *What to listen for:* Look for a collaborative intake process where staff actively ask for family insights and share them across the daily care team.
2. **Q02 · Daily Care & Legacies™:** What does 24-hour on-site staff support look like during the day and overnight, and how are routine assessments handled?
   - *Why it matters:* Understanding how staff assist with bathing, dressing, grooming, transfers, and nighttime reassurance gives families confidence in everyday safety.
   - *What to listen for:* Clear, plain-language explanations of how staff respond to resident needs and how families are updated when care plans adjust.
3. **Q03 · Suites & Personalization:** May we walk through available Private Suites, Companion Units, and Jack & Jill layouts to see the private bathrooms and lighting in person?
   - *Why it matters:* Seeing the physical scale, natural light, and bathroom layout helps you picture which suite format best matches your loved one’s personality.
   - *What to listen for:* How companion matching is thoughtfully approached for shared suites and which apartment features (such as thermostat and emergency alert) apply.
4. **Q04 · Suites & Personalization:** What favorite furniture, framed family photographs, or familiar keepsakes do you recommend bringing to help the suite feel like home?
   - *Why it matters:* Familiar visual cues and textures provide immediate emotional grounding during the transition into a new environment.
   - *What to listen for:* Practical guidance on arranging personal items safely and comfortably before your loved one arrives.
5. **Q05 · Dining & Community Life:** How are chef-prepared meals served each day, and how do you accommodate personal food preferences or dietary needs?
   - *Why it matters:* Shared mealtimes are a daily anchor of warmth, nutrition, and social connection.
   - *What to listen for:* Attentive table service, gentle mealtime cues, and flexibility for resident tastes and family dining visits.
6. **Q06 · Dining & Community Life:** How does the Activity Director adapt daily games, music, art, gentle fitness, and courtyard time for different energy levels?
   - *Why it matters:* Meaningful engagement should feel inviting rather than overwhelming, offering both group camaraderie and quiet one-on-one moments.
   - *What to listen for:* Examples of how staff gently invite participation while respecting when a resident prefers quiet rest in the lounge or patio.
7. **Q07 · Cost, Assessment & Timing:** Following a care assessment, what services are included in the monthly rate versus billed as optional conveniences?
   - *Why it matters:* Separating core inclusions (suite, meals, 24-hour staff, housekeeping, laundry, activities) from optional services prevents surprises.
   - *What to listen for:* A transparent walk-through of how care assessments, suite formats, and optional services (like salon/spa) are structured.
8. **Q08 · Cost, Assessment & Timing:** If we are considering a short-term Respite Care stay or planning a move in the coming weeks, what are the steps and timeline?
   - *Why it matters:* Knowing the sequence from initial conversation and assessment to suite preparation helps families coordinate smoothly.
   - *What to listen for:* Clear guidance on required documentation, assessment scheduling, and current suite availability at 995 East Market Street.
