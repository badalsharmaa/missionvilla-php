## Section 02: The 4 Factors That Shape Senior Living Costs (`financial-planning.factors`)

* **Section Key:** `fp-factors`
* **Status:** Enabled
* **Order:** 20
* **Eyebrow:** Transparent Structure
* **Main Heading:** The 4 Factors That Shape Senior Living Costs
* **Description:** Rather than publishing an arbitrary flat fee, Mission Villa structures monthly pricing around four clear, verifiable factors. Understanding how these factors interact helps your family plan thoughtfully.

### 4 Pricing Factors
1. **Factor 01 · Individualized Support Level — Assessment-Informed Personal Care Needs** (*Tailored around the daily support your loved one actually needs*)
   - *Explanation:* No two residents experience Alzheimer’s disease or dementia in the exact same way. Rather than publishing a one-size-fits-all rate online, Mission Villa conducts a thoughtful care assessment to understand your loved one’s daily routines, personal care needs, mobility, and Legacies™ Memory Care preferences.
   - *What We Review Together:* Level of daily assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, and personal hygiene; Escort and transfer support to the dining room, courtyard, and daily activities; Routine reassessments over time so care plans remain aligned as daily support needs evolve.
   - *Visit Question:* "How does the initial care assessment work, and how are families notified if care needs change over time?"
   - *Related Link:* Explore Legacies™ Memory Care (`/memory-care`)

2. **Factor 02 · Accommodation Format — Suite Layout & Shared vs. Private Living** (*Private Suite, Companion Unit, or Jack & Jill floor-plan formats*)
   - *Explanation:* Accommodation costs vary depending on the suite format your family selects. Some families prefer the quiet sanctuary of an individual Private Suite, while others value the shared camaraderie and cost structure of a Companion Unit or Jack & Jill arrangement—each featuring an attached private bathroom.
   - *What We Review Together:* Private Suite with dedicated sleeping/living area and attached private bathroom; Companion Unit (Shared Suite) designed for comforting companionship and shared monthly value; Jack & Jill format connecting distinct sleeping quarters with an adjoining private bathroom.
   - *Visit Question:* "May we compare current availability and monthly rates across Private, Companion, and Jack & Jill suites?"
   - *Related Link:* Compare Suites & Floor Plans (`/suites-and-floor-plans`)

3. **Factor 03 · Program & Stay Duration — Long-Term Memory Care vs. Short-Term Respite Stays** (*Ongoing residential living or temporary caregiver relief*)
   - *Explanation:* Financial planning looks different when preparing for a permanent move into Memory Care versus arranging a short-term Respite Care stay of a few days to several weeks while a primary family caregiver rests, travels, or recovers.
   - *What We Review Together:* Monthly residential planning for ongoing Legacies™ Memory Care living; Short-term Respite Care stay structure, furnished suite availability, and minimum stay lengths; How a temporary Respite Care stay can serve as a gentle, low-pressure introduction to Mission Villa.
   - *Visit Question:* "How is short-term Respite Care billed compared with full-time Memory Care residency?"
   - *Related Link:* Learn About Respite Care Stays (`/respite-care`)

4. **Factor 04 · Clarity on Everyday vs. Optional Items — Included Community Living vs. Personal Add-Ons** (*Understanding what is part of base care versus optional conveniences*)
   - *Explanation:* A clear financial conversation separates everyday community inclusions—such as housing, three daily chef-prepared meals, housekeeping, laundry, and daily activities—from optional personal services or specialized requests that may carry separate charges.
   - *What We Review Together:* Core residential inclusions (suite, utilities, dining, housekeeping, laundry, activities); Individual care support tiers determined during your loved one’s care assessment; Confirming which conveniences (such as on-site salon/spa appointments or specialized transport) involve separate fees.
   - *Visit Question:* "Can you walk us through a sample resident agreement so we see which services are base inclusions versus optional?"
   - *Related Link:* Review Services & Amenities (`/services-and-amenities`)
