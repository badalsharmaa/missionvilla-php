## Section 05: FAQs (`for-families.faq`)
- **Section Key:** `ff-faq`
1. **What care does Mission Villa offer?** Legacies™ Memory Care and short-term Respite Care.
2. **What suite options are there?** Private suites, companion units, and Jack & Jill formats are described in the source materials. Ask about current openings. (`/suites-and-floor-plans`)
3. **Can I find prices online?** Request current pricing based on the care and accommodation being considered. (`/financial-planning`)
4. **How can we arrange a tour?** Call (650) 756-1995 or submit a visit request.
