2. **Suite choice:** Private, companion, or another available accommodation.

## Section 06: FAQs (`financial-planning.faq`)
- **Section Key:** `fp-faq`
1. **Can I get a price before visiting?** Ask the team for current starting information and the steps needed for a personalized quote.
2. **Will the cost depend on the suite?** It can. Room type and assessed assistance needs may affect pricing.
3. **What about insurance or benefits?** Policy coverage and eligibility vary. Ask your insurer about long-term care benefits and discuss documentation with our team. Do not assume Mission Villa participates in a particular payment or assistance program without confirmation.
