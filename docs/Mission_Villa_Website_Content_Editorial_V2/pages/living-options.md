## Section 05: FAQs (`living-options.faq`)
- **Section Key:** `lo-faq`
1. **Does Mission Villa offer independent living or traditional assisted living?** The submitted materials describe Mission Villa as focused on Memory Care and Respite Care, not separate independent-living or assisted-living programs.
2. **How do we know which option is appropriate?** Talk with the team about the length of support needed and your loved one's daily care requirements. The team can explain its assessment process.
