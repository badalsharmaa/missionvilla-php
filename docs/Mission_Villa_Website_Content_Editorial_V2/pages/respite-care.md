- **Section Key:** `rc-scenarios`
- **Eyebrow:** Is Respite Right for Us?
- **Heading:** A short stay can help in different ways.
1. **A break for caregivers:** Time to recharge while a loved one stays in a supportive community.
2. **Family travel or commitments:** Care while you're away for work, travel, or personal responsibilities.
3. **Exploring community life:** An opportunity to experience Mission Villa before considering a longer-term move.
- **Note:** If your loved one is leaving a hospital or rehabilitation setting, ask the team about admission requirements and whether their needs can be safely supported. Respite Care is not a substitute for skilled nursing or medical rehabilitation.
